## 1.0.0-beta.1

First public beta.

- Sign in with Claude in the browser (OAuth with PKCE), read-only scope `user:profile`; tokens encrypted with the
  Android Keystore.
- Session and weekly limits with reset times, per-model limits, a usage chart with pace and projection, and an
  estimate of the full sessions left this week.
- Ring, Bars, Chart and Dashboard widgets, resizable and customizable, following the system theme.
- Background refresh every 5 minutes by default (up to an hour), redraws at limit resets, optional notifications.
- English and Russian.
